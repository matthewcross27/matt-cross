
# Personal Website Experience Requirements

## Project Goal

Create a personal website that feels like entering a living sketchbook: hand-drawn, tactile, cinematic, and personal.

The website should communicate personality and interests while functioning as a professional portfolio and project showcase. The experience should feel memorable because of its atmosphere and storytelling, not because of visual novelty alone.

The site should leave visitors with the feeling:

> "I learned who Matt is through an experience, not through a collection of pages."

The design language and motion system are part of the core identity of the website rather than decorative layers added afterward.

## Primary Outcomes

1. Communicate personality quickly and distinctly.
2. Preserve content clarity.
3. Create emotional continuity.
4. Create moments of delight without becoming distracting.
5. Present hobbies and interests as extensions of identity.

## Visual Identity

The visual language should resemble a sketchbook or notebook that has come to life.

Characteristics:

- Off-white paper-like surfaces
- Visible texture and tactile quality
- Pencil or graphite-inspired marks
- Hand-drawn imperfections
- Simple stick figures and doodles
- Sparse, intentional use of color
- Editorial rather than cartoon-like composition

Should feel:

- Human
- Intentional
- Calm
- Warm
- Thoughtful

Should avoid:

- Childish
- Cartoonish
- Gimmicky
- Overdesigned
- Artificially cute

## Motion Principles

Motion is central to the experience.

Motion should feel:

- Natural
- Deliberate
- Physical
- Smooth
- Organic

Motion should resemble:

- Camera movement
- Drawings coming alive
- Objects existing within physical space
- Small environmental reactions

Motion should avoid:

- Constant looping effects
- Attention-seeking movement
- Decorative motion without purpose

## Scroll Experience

Scrolling should feel like moving through a continuous world.

Users should feel:

- Progression
- Anticipation
- Continuity

Avoid:

- Abrupt transitions
- Isolated animation blocks
- Slide-deck behavior

## Content Hierarchy

Priority order:

1. Identity and story
2. Projects and work
3. Supporting personal context
4. Motion and visual details

Visual elements should reinforce content rather than interrupt it.

## Hobby Integration Requirements

Hobbies should communicate:

- Values
- Personality
- Habits
- Mindset
- Lessons

The site should connect hobbies to personal and professional identity rather than treating them as separate categories.

## Story Flow Requirements

Beginning:

- Establish personality
- Establish tone
- Create curiosity

Middle:

- Reveal interests
- Reveal projects
- Deepen understanding

End:

- Create closure
- Leave a lasting impression
- Provide clear next actions

## Interaction Requirements

Interactions should feel:

- Responsive
- Subtle
- Tactile
- Physical

Should avoid:

- Game-like interactions
- Hyperactive reactions
- Repetitive effects

## Accessibility Requirements

- Content remains accessible without animation
- Reduced motion preferences are respected
- Readability remains high
- Keyboard navigation works
- Motion enhances content rather than carrying meaning

## Performance Requirements

- Smooth interactions
- Smooth scrolling
- No visible stutter
- No heavy-loading feeling

Content quality takes priority over visual effects.

## Success Criteria

Success:

- "It felt like opening someone's sketchbook."
- "The motion felt natural."
- "The site felt alive."
- "I wanted to keep scrolling."

Failure:

- "Cool animation demo."
- "Too much happening."
- "The stick figures felt gimmicky."
- "I couldn't focus on the content."
